The Department for Transport's official images of the British Isles' traffic signs, named by their TSRGD 2016 diagram
number (https://www.gov.uk/guidance/traffic-sign-images): 610 KEEP LEFT, 616 NO ENTRY, 816 NO THROUGH ROAD.
`python3 tools/build_uk_signs.py` turns them into the mod's sign faces.
